## 12. Reliable client care.

Our working standard is 100% reliability: own every promise and follow-up.

**Acknowledge** — Understand the client’s goal and thank them for feedback.

**Stay helpful** — If unsure: “We will look into it.” Check before promising a solution.

**Set the next update** — Agree a realistic time. Update before it, even if work continues.

**Close the loop** — Explain limits honestly and offer useful alternatives.

Promise a reliable follow-up. Only promise the solution after the team has checked it.

**When unsure**

“Let me look into this.”

**Example client reply**

“Thanks for your feedback. I’ll check with our team and update you by Thursday, 29 October, 15:00 Bali time (WITA).”

**Acknowledging feedback — template**

“Thanks for your feedback. I understand you’d like [goal]. I’ll check [issue] with our team and get back to you by [date, time, time zone].”

**An uncertain request**

“We will look into it. I’ll check what’s possible with our team and get back to you by [date, time, time zone].”

**A delay — send before the agreed time**

“A quick update: we’re still checking [issue], and I don’t have a confirmed answer yet. I’ll update you again by [date, time, time zone].”

**If the exact request cannot work**

“We’ve checked [request]. [Specific constraint] means that approach won’t work. We can offer [alternative] to help you achieve [goal]. I’ll send the details by [date, time, time zone].”

**Requesting client input**

“To confirm [decision], could you send [specific item]? Once I have it, I’ll review it with our team. I’ll update you on progress by [date, time, time zone], even if we’re still waiting for the item.”

**An additional request**

“Thanks for the suggestion. I’ll check it with Raoul and confirm any effect on the agreed work, timing or cost by [date, time, time zone].”

**Closing a resolved issue — after testing**

“We’ve fixed [issue] and checked [relevant customer journey]. You can review it here: [link]. I’ll check in with you by [date, time, time zone] to confirm it works as expected.”

### Presenter notes

Reliability means keeping commitments and communicating early. An update deadline is not a promise that work will be finished. Betzy owns client follow-up. Check product decisions with Raoul, design/development with Mohammed, and copy/SEO with Ryan. Choose realistic update times with the team. Keep replies warm, brief and concrete; explain what the request means for customers. Never blame a teammate or provider. Only say “fixed” or “checked” after the work and tests are complete.
