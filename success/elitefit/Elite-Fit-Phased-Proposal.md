## 07 — SEO page scope and deliverables

After Elite Fit approves the website, we plan each new page around one agreed topic and a separate search. The same steps apply to package pages and optional extra pages.

1. **Research searches:** See what people search for and what they hope to find.
2. **Choose one topic:** Choose a clear topic for the page and check that another page does not cover the same thing.
3. **Agree the content:** Set who the page is for, what it should answer, the facts we can confirm, useful links and the next step.
4. **Plan the page:** Agree the headings and sections, and decide where proof and next steps belong.
5. **Write the copy:** Write clear, original copy and check the facts with Elite Fit before approval.
6. **Build the page:** Create an editable WordPress page that works on phones and computers, with useful images and links.
7. **Set page details:** Add a page title and short description, choose its web address and set how search engines can include it.
8. **Review and publish:** Check the copy, phone layout, links and search settings. Publish after approval and check that search engines can reach the page.

Submitting a sitemap to a search engine requires separate approval.

## 08 — Maintain and improve search rankings through ongoing optimisation

Every Phase 2 package includes monthly tracking, one priority improvement and a report. Search positions, traffic and sales depend on search engines, the offer and the visitor experience; they are not guaranteed.

- **Search results and page visits.** See which pages people find and visit, and where search results are changing. **Monthly output:** A short list of findings and pages to review.
- **Page text and links.** Check page copy, titles, descriptions, headings and links between pages for improvements. **Monthly output:** A short list of text and link changes, in priority order.
- **Technical checks.** Check that links work, pages load quickly and search engines can reach the right pages. **Monthly output:** A list of technical problems and the order to fix them.
- **Routes to enquiries.** Review how visitors move from a page to an enquiry or booking, using available website data. **Monthly output:** Suggested changes to navigation, next-step buttons or enquiry steps.
- **Make and check one change.** Make one agreed priority improvement each month, then check the result. **Monthly output:** The change made and a record of the follow-up check.
- **Monthly report and next steps.** Report the pages published, available search results and enquiries where tracking is in place. List completed changes, open issues and next steps. **Monthly output:** A monthly report with results, changes and next steps.
