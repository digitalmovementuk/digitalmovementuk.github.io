02 — Starting point

CEx case study: the starting point

CEx began in 2025 on a new domain, so Google had almost nothing to show. The name made it harder too, because a search for "cex" brings up CeX, an electronics retailer, and CEX.IO, a crypto exchange. So the site had to show up for what CEx does and where it works. Our goal for CEx: search as a steady way to win qualified first conversations with new clients.

03 — Research first

What we did in the CEx case study: research first

1 Step 1

Research and strategy, June–July 2026. First, deep keyword research into which search terms are commercially relevant for CEx (Semrush). Next, we filtered them by commercial intent and clustered them into focus search phrases, each backed by associated keywords. That gave 62 focus phrases, one page each.

From 4,250 keywords to 62 pages

  1. 4,250 keywords, 7 service lines
  2. Filtered by commercial intent
  3. Clustered into focus phrases
  4. 62 focus search phrases
  5. 62 pages, one per phrase
Two kinds of pages

National pages take terms with higher keyword difficulty and more competition, whereas strategic local pages take easier ones.

  • National: digitalisation consultancy
  • National: process optimisation consultancy
  • National: AI chatbot consultancy
  • Local: project management consultancy Munich
  • Local: IT consultancy Hamburg
2
Step 2

From brief to optimised page. Every page then went through the same five stages.

  1. An SEO page brief holding all the information for that page
  2. German copy, reworked page by page with feedback from Johannes Reusch
  3. On-page and technical SEO: meta data, slugs and the like
  4. An opening paragraph for AI search that answers the reader's question, and the AI's, end to end
  5. Trust, experience and authority signals: the pages show CEx's deliverables, results and its own working method

Design and build of the CEx website

10 — Takeaways and next steps

What this means for your business

Takeaway one

When big companies already own a short brand name in search, the way in is through services and cities.

Takeaway two

Give interested visitors a clear route from finding you to contacting you. Explain who you help, what you can do for them and how to take the next step, so making an enquiry feels straightforward.
