How we use your details

Who we are. Remind MHT Limited, trading as Re;mind Mental Health Therapies, is the data controller for this website and for your care. Company no. 14277510. Registered office: Office 43 Steele House, Solent Business Park, Whiteley, Fareham, Hampshire PO15 7EP. ICO registration ZB626082. Data protection queries: enquiries@remindmht.com.

What this form does. Pressing the button opens an email on your own device, addressed to enquiries@remindmht.com, with what you typed. You decide whether to send it; this website does not store your details. If no email app opens, call us on 07575 040 401 instead.

Why we use your details. When you contact us, we use your name, phone number, email address and message to reply and to arrange an introductory consultation. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and, if you go on to have therapy, the steps needed to provide that care. Anything you tell us about your health is special category data. We hold it only with your explicit consent and to provide your care.

How long we keep it. If you do not become a client, we delete your enquiry after 12 months. Client records are kept securely for 7 years after your last session, then deleted. Notes are held on an encrypted, UK-hosted system.

Your rights. You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and ask for it in a portable format. Email enquiries@remindmht.com and we will reply within one month.

Complaints. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information or your care, email Susie Deacon, Founder, via enquiries@remindmht.com; we reply within 5 working days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113, and, for professional matters, to your therapist's professional body BACP or HCPC.
