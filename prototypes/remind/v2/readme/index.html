How do people look for a therapist today?

They ask a question. The clearest, most trustworthy answer is shown first.

Someone decides they would like help. They pick up their phone and type their worry, or the therapy they have heard of, and a place: “counselling in Fareham”, “EMDR therapy near me”, “does VR therapy work for a fear of heights?”. More and more people ask an AI assistant the same thing in a full sentence.

Google and the assistants then do one job. They look for the page that answers that exact question most clearly and most trustworthily, and they show it first. Most people never look past the first few results. So the question for every practice is simple: when someone near Whiteley asks for the help you offer, is Re;mind the clear answer?

Each of the three pages is built to be that answer for one question.

While we were looking

Three small things we noticed on your current website

All three are quick to put right, and each one helps Google trust what it finds.

  • The “contact us” link on your questions page leads to an error page, so some people who want to get in touch cannot.
  • There is no privacy page. Visitors and Google expect one, and it belongs alongside your registration with the Information Commissioner’s Office.
  • The postcode on the website (PO15 7FP) differs from the one at Companies House (PO15 7EP), and the building is spelt Steel House on one and Steele House on the other. Consistent details help Google trust the address.
