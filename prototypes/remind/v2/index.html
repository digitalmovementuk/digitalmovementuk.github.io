Re;mind offers mental health therapy in Hampshire from calm, private rooms in Whiteley. We are a short drive from Fareham, Southampton and Portsmouth. If travelling is hard, we also work online by video. [Either way, you see the same therapist each time.]

Featured approach

What is VR therapy, and who is it for?

Some fears are hard to practise in real life. Nobody wants to stand on a car park roof on their first attempt. In VR therapy you wear a headset and step into a lifelike scene. Your therapist adjusts how high, how close or how crowded it becomes, and only when you are ready.

You set the pace, you can stop at any time, and we repeat each step until it feels manageable.

Case study

What happened when one client faced her fear of heights?

Claire, 41, from Locks Heath, had avoided car park roofs, footbridges and glass lifts for thirty years. Over seven weekly appointments she spent 169 minutes in a headset, moving from a first-floor balcony to a glass-floor platform.

Her Acrophobia Questionnaire score fell from 92 to 43 out of 160. At Easter she went up the Spinnaker Tower with her children, and at her three-month review her score was 37. Improvement, not a cure: glass floors and open edges still frighten her.
