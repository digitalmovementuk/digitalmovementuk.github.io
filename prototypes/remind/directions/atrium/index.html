Re;mind is a small, independent practice in Whiteley, Hampshire, offering counselling, EMDR, hypnotherapy, VR therapy and coaching to adults and young adults. We see people in person in Whiteley, near Fareham, or online by video. A brief introductory consultation helps you choose an approach, and we are open seven days a week, with weekday evenings until 10pm.

Therapy shaped around you, for adults and young adults, in person in Whiteley or online

Re;mind offers mental health therapy in Hampshire from calm, private rooms in Whiteley, a short drive from Fareham, Southampton and Portsmouth. We are a small, independent practice founded by Susie Deacon, with counsellors and a clinical psychologist under one roof.

If travelling is hard, we also work online by video. We listen first, then agree a plan that helps you feel steadier.
