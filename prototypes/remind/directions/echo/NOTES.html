## 5. What still differs from the reference, and why

1. Wordmark proportion and case: "Re;mind" has 7 characters against 15 and is set in the brand's own case rather than caps, so spanning the same width makes the type larger than the reference's (box ≈ 200 px vs 126 px at 1440; ≈ 70 px vs 32 px on iPhone). Keeping the edge-to-edge composition was preferred over keeping the cap height.
2. Fonts are free substitutes (Archivo 580 ↔ GT America Standard Medium; Newsreader 400 ↔ GT Super Text Book). Archivo sets slightly wider, so line breaks differ a little.
3. Hero is a graded double-exposure still (therapy room × woodland) with slow zoom, drifting dappled light and grain flicker, not a video; all three double exposures are landscapes/interiors rather than portraits (people-free by house rule).
4. Six numbered services instead of four and different copy lengths: the page is longer (5696 px vs 5376 px at 1440; 5801 px vs 5265 px on iPhone).
5. The Lottie wordmark drawing is approximated (clip-path wipe with a baseline stroke); the phone header fade window is fitted from three samples.
6. The reference's scroll-triggered newsletter pop-up is not replicated.
