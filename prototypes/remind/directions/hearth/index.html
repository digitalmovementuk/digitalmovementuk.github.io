Mental health therapy, shaped around you.

An independent practice in Whiteley, Hampshire

We listen first, then agree a plan

Re;mind offers mental health therapy in Hampshire from calm, private rooms in Whiteley, a short drive from Fareham, Southampton and Portsmouth. We are a small, independent practice founded by Susie Deacon, with counsellors and a clinical psychologist under one roof. If travelling is hard, we also work online by video.
