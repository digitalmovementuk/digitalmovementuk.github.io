At Re;mind, therapy is not one size fits all, so there are no scripts or fixed timelines.

We listen first, then agree an approach, a pace and a frequency that fit your life. A brief introductory consultation helps you choose, and the number of sessions is worked out with you.

talk to us.

one calm practice.

Six approaches under one roof in Whiteley: counselling, EMDR, hypnotherapy, VR therapy, coaching and therapist mentoring.

Re;mind is a small, independent practice founded by Susie Deacon, with counsellors and a clinical psychologist under one roof. People come to us with stress, anxiety, low mood, trauma and grief. We listen first, then agree a plan that helps you feel steadier. Pacing, session timing and the number of sessions are worked out individually, and payment can be discussed.

You can choose how we meet:

face to face.

A sit-down in Whiteley.

Calm, private rooms at Steel House on Solent Business Park, a short drive from Fareham, Southampton and Portsmouth. An accessible building with a lift and free parking at the door.

online.

The same session, by video.

If travelling is hard, we also work online by video. Pacing, session timing and the number of sessions are worked out with you, whichever way we meet.

vr therapy.

Face a fear step by step, in a headset.

Face a feared situation step by step in a headset, with your therapist beside you. For specific fears such as heights, crowds, open spaces or spiders.
