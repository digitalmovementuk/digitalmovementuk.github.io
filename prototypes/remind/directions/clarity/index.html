Which kind of mental health therapy in Hampshire suits you?

That depends on what you bring, so here is what each of our six approaches is for.

  • Counselling

    Talk through what is weighing on you with a counsellor who blends methods such as CBT, DBT, transactional analysis and Gestalt. For stress, grief, trauma, anxiety and low mood.

  • EMDR

    Helps the mind file away memories that still feel raw, using guided eye movements or tapping while you recall the event from a safe distance. Often used for trauma, for example after an accident.

  • Hypnotherapy

    Deep relaxation and guided imagery, while you stay in control throughout. For anxiety, fears, habits such as smoking, pain, IBS and nerves before medical treatment.

  • VR therapy

    Face a feared situation step by step in a headset, with your therapist beside you. For specific fears such as heights, crowds, open spaces or spiders.

    How VR therapy works

  • Coaching

    Forward-looking support for career, life, health, study and relationships. Unlike counselling, coaching starts from where you want to be.

  • Therapist mentoring

    One-to-one mentoring, group sessions and guest speakers for counsellors, whether in training or in practice, face to face in Whiteley.
