6. Compliance flags

Tick everything that applies. Each one adds required page elements.

  • German site → Impressum and Datenschutzerklärung required
  • UK site → company details in footer, cookie consent
  • Medical / dental → HWG restrictions on claims and before/after imagery
  • Financial / legal services → regulated claim wording
  • Any lead form → DSGVO/GDPR consent checkbox, named legal basis, privacy link
  • Cookies beyond strictly necessary → consent banner before any tracking fires
  • SEO or service landing page → the 242-item seo-page-qa-gate applies
