How to use this. This is the only place final copy lives. Not Slack, not email, not a comment in the design file. It is also the one deliverable that never closes — it stays live past launch as the record of what the site says. Two versions exist. v1 is written from the blueprint, before layout. v2 is the same copy fitted to real components once the character budgets from 05 are known. Never write v1 to an imaginary word count. Standards that apply: low-cognitive-load-copy (readability, word budgets, front-loading), web-copy-craft (sounding human, section headers), and the plain-language rule — a fifteen-year-old must understand it at a glance.

6. Claims and compliance

Every factual claim needs a source. Regulated sectors need more.

Claim on the page Source / evidence Verified by Date
  • No unverifiable superlatives ("the best", "number one") without proof
  • No invented statistics, awards, client counts or testimonials
  • Testimonials are real, attributed, and permission granted
  • Medical/dental: no prohibited claims, no non-compliant before/after imagery (HWG)
  • Prices, guarantees and turnaround times confirmed with the client in writing
